Lupa ("magnifying glass" in Croatian) is a bilingual blog, in Croatian and English, that puts small listed companies under the lens. These are companies usually worth less than two or three billion dollars, and they mostly make the news when their share price jumps or drops by a third. We want to write about them in between.

What we cover

  • Small and micro caps. Companies the big banks rarely cover, which are often more interesting technologically than their market value suggests.
  • Bioengineering. Synthetic biology, cell and gene therapies, AI in drug discovery and lab automation.
  • Quantum computing and deep tech. Quantum hardware, sensing, photonics and software, with a focus on what is actually being sold today.
  • Ties to large companies. A small company is often worth what its relationships with big pharma, tech, defence and cloud companies are worth. So we check which deals are real contracts and which are press-release "collaborations".
  • Markets. Rates, indices and sector rotation, always asking what they mean for small companies.

How we work

Every analysis starts from primary sources: SEC EDGAR filings, company releases and earnings calls. For prices and indices we use publicly available delayed data, and every price is labelled with its source, session and time. Figures we calculate ourselves, such as cash runway or value-to-revenue multiples, are clearly marked as Lupa calculations. If we cannot verify a number, we do not publish it.

Every deep dive treats risks as seriously as opportunities: dilution, clinical failure, dependence on government contracts and long quantum timelines.

What Lupa is not

Lupa is not an investment firm, does not manage money and does not give investment advice. Its articles are informational and analytical. Before any investment decision, do your own research or consult a licensed adviser. See our disclaimer.

Publishing

Lupa publishes on weekdays: a market wrap after the US session, an "On the radar" watchlist and regular company deep dives. Every piece exists in both languages.
