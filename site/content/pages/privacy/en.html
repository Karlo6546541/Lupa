Lupa is an independent bilingual small-cap research blog. We try to collect as little as possible: you can read Lupa without registering, we use no analytics, advertising or tracking cookies, and we do not sell personal data. Below is what we do process and why.

Who is responsible

The controller of personal data is Karlo Švenda, Lupa research desk, Međimurje, Croatia. For any privacy question or to exercise your rights, write to {{contact_email}}.

What we process and why

  • Visiting the website. Like any web server, our host (Vercel) technically processes your IP address, the time, the requested address and browser details with each request, and keeps them briefly in logs to deliver the site, keep it secure and fix errors. Basis: legitimate interest in a secure, working website.
  • Language choice. Your browser's local storage (localStorage) remembers whether you chose Croatian or English. It stays on your device and is not sent to us.
  • Web subscription. Payment happens on Stripe's hosted checkout page (Stripe Checkout). Stripe collects your email address, card or wallet details (Google Pay, Apple Pay) and billing address as needed. Lupa never sees or stores your card number. We receive a customer identifier and the subscription status from Stripe so we can unlock the analyses for you. Basis: performance of a contract and legal obligations (accounting and tax). Note: web payments are not switched on yet.
  • Subscription cookies. We only use strictly necessary cookies, with no tracking or advertising: __Host-lupa_checkout (short-lived, at most one hour; links your return from Stripe's checkout to your browser) and __Host-lupa_session (signed, HttpOnly and Secure, up to one year; holds only the Stripe customer identifier and the time of the last subscription check). They are set only when you start a subscription, and signing out of a device deletes them. Strictly necessary cookies do not require consent.
  • Mobile app. On your device the app remembers your language, the last downloaded copy of the public content and, if you subscribe, the store's purchase receipt. In-app subscriptions are bought through the App Store or Google Play; Lupa does not receive your card details or your Apple ID or Google account. To unlock the analyses, the app sends the purchase token to our server, which checks it with Apple or Google. We do not store that token in a database — Lupa has no user database. Basis: performance of a contract. The app contains no analytics or advertising SDKs.
  • Newsletter. If you sign up, we use your email address and chosen language only to send the newsletter. We never sell or share it, and you can unsubscribe at any time. Until automatic sign-up is switched on, a sign-up arrives as an email to the editors. Basis: your consent, which you can withdraw whenever you like.
  • When you write to us. We use your email address and message to reply. Basis: legitimate interest in answering, or performance of a contract if it concerns your subscription.

Who helps us (processors)

  • Vercel Inc. — hosting for the website and server functions.
  • Stripe — billing and subscription management on the web (customer portal: update card, invoices, cancel). For payment data Stripe also acts partly as an independent controller under its own privacy policy.
  • Apple and Google — purchase and billing of in-app subscriptions through the App Store and Google Play. For those purchases they are the seller and process your data under their own privacy policies.

If we add a new service that processes personal data, such as a newsletter provider, we will list it here.

Transfers outside the EU

These providers may process some data outside the European Economic Area, mainly in the United States. Such transfers rely on GDPR safeguards such as the EU–US Data Privacy Framework or the European Commission's standard contractual clauses.

How long we keep data

  • Server logs: briefly, as long as Vercel keeps them.
  • Subscription and payment records: while the subscription runs and afterwards for as long as accounting and tax law requires.
  • Newsletter email address: until you unsubscribe.
  • Correspondence: as long as needed to deal with your request.
  • Data in the app and browser: on your device until you delete it or uninstall the app.

Your rights

Under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) you have the right to access your data, have it corrected or erased, restrict processing, object to processing based on legitimate interest, data portability, and to withdraw consent at any time. Write to {{contact_email}}. If you believe we process your data unlawfully, you can lodge a complaint with the Croatian Personal Data Protection Agency (AZOP, azop.hr) or the data protection authority where you live.

Children
