Editorial rules

  • Lupa keeps full editorial control. Partners do not approve copy before publication.
  • We do not publish paid stock promotion, “price targets” or buy recommendations — not from issuers, not from third parties.
  • We do not take money from a company whose stock we are analysing in the same period.
  • Sponsored content is never disguised as independent research.
