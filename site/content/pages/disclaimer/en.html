Risks

Investing in stocks, and especially in small-company stocks, carries high risk, including the possible loss of the entire amount invested. Small caps can be illiquid and highly volatile. Biotech companies face clinical and regulatory risk, and quantum computing companies face long and uncertain technology timelines. Loss-making companies often fund themselves by issuing new shares, which dilutes existing shareholders. Past performance does not guarantee future results.

Data and accuracy

Prices and index data come from publicly available, delayed sources (mainly Yahoo Finance), and every price is labelled with its source and time. Prices in articles refer to the stated session and are not updated after publication. Company financials come from official filings and releases. We aim for accuracy but do not guarantee the completeness or accuracy of third-party data, and we accept no liability for decisions made on the basis of published content.
